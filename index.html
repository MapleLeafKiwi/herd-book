How this page works
  • Every animal on the ranch. Search by tag, brand, group or notes.
  • Tap a group button to see just that group. Tap an animal to see or change its details.
  • + Add animal for a new one. The orange collar symbol means its collar is on.
  • Sold animals are hidden. Tick Show sold animals to see them.
  • New group: tap Manage groups, then + New group. Type the name, tick the cattle and tap Move. The group is saved once cattle are in it.
  • Move cattle: tap Select, tick the animals (or type collar numbers like 1-20, 35), then tap Move to group.
  • Manage groups also adds cattle to a group, renames it, or empties it.

How this page works
  • Pick a collar and an animal, in either order, then tap Fit.
  • Pick an animal and its collar from last season is picked for you. Pick a collar and the animal it was last on is picked for you.
  • Lost ear tag? Pick Animal not known (lost tag). Work out which one later on the Collars tab.
  • Type the collar number or tap Photo to read it off the collar. It jumps to the top of the list.
  • Made a mistake? Tap Undo on the message that pops up.

How this page works
  • Your list of collars and where each one is.
  • Fit collars to put collars on cattle at the chute.
  • Fitted: on an animal now. Not fitted: ready to use. Lost: missing or out of use.
  • Tap a collar to mark it lost or found, take it off its animal, or add its eShepherd serial.
  • + Add collars to enter numbers. A range like 1-150 works.
  • Collars off for the year? Start new season marks them all as not fitted.
Fit collars
Collars off for the year?
Start a new season to mark every fitted collar as not fitted. Each animal remembers its collar, so when you fit collars again it’s matched with the same one. Collars on unknown animals are cleared too. Fitted right now: 0.
