How this page works
  • Every animal on the ranch. Search by tag, brand, group or notes.

How this page works
  • For the chute when fitting collars.
  • Type the brand or collar number, or tap Photo to read the number off the collar.
  • Needs collar shows the animals still to do. All shows everything.
  • Tap Collar fitted ✓ once it’s on. Fit collar gives a collar to an animal that doesn’t have one yet.
How this page works
  • Your list of collars and where each one is.
  • + Add collars to enter numbers. A range like 1-150 works.
  • Unused: not given to an animal. Not fitted: given but not on yet. Fitted: on the animal.
  • Tap a collar to give it to an animal, add its eShepherd serial, or mark it lost.
  • Collars off for the year? Start new season marks them all as off.
Collars off for the year?
Start a new season to mark every fitted collar as not fitted. Each cow keeps her collar number, so she gets the same collar next time and shows up under “Needs collar” in the Yard. Fitted right now: 0.

  • Which bull went in with which cows, and when calves are due.
